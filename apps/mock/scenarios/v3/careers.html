Announcement: Acme Analytics raises $20M Series B

On April 28, 2026 we closed a $20 million Series B led by Accel, with participation from existing investors Index Ventures, Cherry Ventures, and Y Combinator. This brings total funding to $28 million since the company was founded in 2023. Over the next twelve months we will grow the team from 38 to 80 people, expand our AI copilot, and open a third office in London by Q4 2026. Read more on the blog.

Work at Acme Analytics

We just closed our Series B and are growing the team from 38 to 80 people over the next twelve months. We hire slowly, pay well, and ship. Most of these roles are open in Berlin, San Francisco, or remote across the EU and US.

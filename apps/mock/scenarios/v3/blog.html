Why we open-sourced our session replay player

Posted February 6, 2026 · Open Source · 4 minute read

Session replay is one of those features customers ask for, never want to build, and never want to pay extra for. We agreed. So we open-sourced our entire player library under MIT. Use it with Acme, use it with Mixpanel, use it standalone — we do not care.

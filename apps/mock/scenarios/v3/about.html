Announcement: Acme Analytics raises $20M Series B

On April 28, 2026 we closed a $20 million Series B led by Accel, with participation from existing investors Index Ventures, Cherry Ventures, and Y Combinator. This brings total funding to $28 million since the company was founded in 2023. Over the next twelve months we will grow the team from 38 to 80 people, expand our AI copilot, and open a third office in London by Q4 2026. Read more on the blog.

About Acme Analytics

Acme Analytics was founded in 2023 by Maya Chen and Jonas Weber, two engineers who had spent the previous five years building product analytics tooling at large SaaS companies. They kept hitting the same wall: the tools designed for marketers were not designed for the engineers who actually shipped the product. So they built one that was.

Our investors

We raised an $8 million seed round in 2024 led by Index Ventures, with participation from Y Combinator, Cherry Ventures, and a small group of angel investors who were product leaders at Stripe, Linear, and Figma.

Where we are

Our headquarters are in Berlin, with a second office in San Francisco. About half the team is remote across Europe and the US. We meet up in person twice a year — once in Berlin in the spring, once in San Francisco in the fall.

How we work

We ship every day, we write things down, and we keep meetings to a minimum. Nobody owns more than two areas of the product at a time. We do not have a roadmap document — we have a public changelog instead, and we try to keep what we ship close to what we promised.

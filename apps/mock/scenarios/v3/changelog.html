Announcement: Acme Analytics raises $20M Series B

On April 28, 2026 we closed a $20 million Series B led by Accel, with participation from existing investors Index Ventures, Cherry Ventures, and Y Combinator. This brings total funding to $28 million since the company was founded in 2023. Over the next twelve months we will grow the team from 38 to 80 people, expand our AI copilot, and open a third office in London by Q4 2026. Read more on the blog.

Changelog

What we shipped, week by week. Subscribe to the RSS feed for updates.

v2.4 — April 7, 2026

  • Introducing AI summaries: every dashboard now ships with an automatic weekly summary that calls out the three most important changes in your funnels and retention curves.
  • New built-in A/B test reporting on the Pro plan with sequential analysis and proper sample-size guidance — no more peeking at p-values.
  • Added a new Enterprise plan with single-tenant deployment, SOC 2 Type II reports, and a 99.99% uptime SLA. Talk to sales for pricing.
  • Funnel editor now supports time-window steps with hourly granularity, not just daily.

v2.3 — March 24, 2026

  • Funnel editor now supports cross-platform steps for iOS, Android, and Web in the same funnel definition.
  • Warehouse sync to BigQuery is now twice as fast for tables over 50 million rows.
  • Fixed a regression where saved dashboards lost their custom date range after browser reload.

v2.2 — March 10, 2026

  • New retention chart with rolling 7, 28, and 90 day cohorts.
  • Slack alerts can now be scoped to a specific dashboard tab.
  • Improved error messages when an event property has the wrong type.

v2.1 — February 24, 2026

  • Added support for SAML SSO via Okta and Azure AD on the Scale plan.
  • Public API rate limits raised to 1,000 requests per minute per workspace.
  • Removed the legacy v1 funnel editor. All workspaces are now on v2.

How we cut event ingestion latency from 12 seconds to under 800ms

Posted March 14, 2026 · Engineering · 9 minute read

When we hit our first billion-event week, our ingestion pipeline started to wobble. The old design queued raw events through Kafka into a Postgres staging table before the warehouse sync. That worked at 100 million events a week, but at 1.2 billion it pushed tail latency past 12 seconds. Here is what we did, what failed, and what finally stuck.
